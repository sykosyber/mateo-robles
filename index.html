About

I design and build creative software. My work moves between software engineering, human-computer interaction, applied machine learning, and computational art.

Software is an electric medium: made of light, malleable, reproducible, never exhausted. All those degrees of freedom eventually collapse to a point — the question of what it can do for a person. Ask too early and imagination turns instrumental. Ask too late and you have built a private palace. I try to ask late enough to still be surprised, and early enough that it still has a door.
